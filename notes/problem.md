# The Problem

My cellular internet connection is inconsistent. I use two SIM cards, MTN and Airtel, and I sincerely do not know which one is worse.

MTN could just decide to be useless for hours, maybe even an entire day, and there is nothing I can do about it unless I change location—which is inconvenient, to say the very least.

I could say the exact same thing about Airtel.

Sometimes, switching Airplane Mode on and off helps. Sometimes, completely turning off my device also helps. But most of the time, nothing works.

To be honest, this challenge did not start with me. It has been one of the major challenges of living in a country like mine.

I have always wanted to know why.

So I decided to build a system that collects information about my cellular connection and helps me understand what is happening when its performance changes.

# The Goal

The specific goal of this application is to continuously collect and correlate cellular network data with my internet performance, so I can identify what changes when my connection becomes slow, unstable, or completely unavailable.

I want the system to help answer questions such as:

* What was happening with my cellular connection when my internet suddenly became slow?
* Did my signal strength, network quality, or connection type change?
* Was I connected to a different cell tower or network technology?
* Are there patterns that explain why my connection works well in one location but poorly in another?
* When my internet goes out without me moving, what actually changed behind the scenes?
* How do MTN and Airtel behave under the same conditions over time?

Ultimately, I want to turn an experience that currently feels random and unpredictable into something I can observe, measure, and understand.

I want to know what really happens behind the scenes when my cellular connection suddenly goes out without me changing location.
